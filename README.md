# Jarvis 2.0 — Phase 1

A voice-first personal assistant that runs in your phone's browser. Tap the mic and talk.

- **Notes**: "Note: call the bank about my card"
- **Reminders**: "Remind me to pay rent tomorrow at 9 am"
- **Budget**: "Paid 15 dollars for groceries", then "How much did I spend this month?"

Everything is saved on your own phone (browser storage). Nothing is sent to a server.
Best in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone). Reminders fire while the page is open.
