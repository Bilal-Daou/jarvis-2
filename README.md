# Jarvis 2.0

A voice-first personal assistant that runs in your phone's browser and installs like an app.
Live: https://bilal-daou.github.io/jarvis-2/

**Talk (or type) to it:**
- Notes: "Note: idea for an app that scans food labels"
- Reminders: "Remind me to call mom every Sunday at 6 pm", "in 20 minutes", "tomorrow at 9 am"
- Spending: "Paid 15 dollars for groceries", "Spent 40 on gas"
- Income: "Got paid 2000 salary", "Received 300 from freelance work"
- Budget: "Set my budget to 1500", "How much did I spend this month?", "What's my balance?"
- Fixes: "Undo last expense", "Delete last note"

**Screens:** Home dashboard, Notes (search, tags, pin, edit), Reminders (repeat, snooze, done), Budget (budget goal, category donut, daily chart, income vs spending), Settings (voice picker, speed, pitch, theme, accent color, currency, backup/restore).

Everything is saved on your own device. Nothing is sent to a server.
